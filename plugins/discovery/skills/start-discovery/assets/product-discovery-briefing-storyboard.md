# Product Discovery Briefing Storyboard

Use this as a narrative pattern. Combine, omit, or expand sections to fit the product and audience.

1. **Title:** product concept, discovery purpose, and date.
2. **The situation:** what people are trying to accomplish and what happens now.
3. **The target opportunity:** the progress worth enabling and why it matters.
4. **Actors and value:** who participates, benefits, buys, operates, approves, or bears risk.
5. **The product thesis:** the strongest current product bet and meaningful non-goals.
6. **The core value journey:** the end-to-end flow from the real-world trigger to a meaningful outcome, showing what the product is responsible for and where people or outside systems take over.
7. **Critical branches and handoffs:** add only the alternate paths, approvals, exceptions, or recovery flows that materially change value or risk. Omit this section when the overview is enough.
8. **Domain and system context:** important information, state, organizations, platforms, and responsibility boundaries without choosing architecture.
9. **Dependencies and research:** current external facts that materially shaped the thesis.
10. **Critical assumptions:** what must be true and what could disprove the product idea.
11. **Next learning move:** the most useful way to reduce the current uncertainty.

For a simple product, use one journey flow and combine related sections. For a complex enterprise product, keep one readable journey overview in the main story and move phase, branch, actor, domain, or dependency views into an appendix.
