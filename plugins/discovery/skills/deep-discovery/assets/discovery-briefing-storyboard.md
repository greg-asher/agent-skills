# Discovery Briefing Storyboard

Use this as a narrative pattern. Combine, omit, or expand sections to fit the application and audience.

1. **Title:** application, discovery purpose, and date.
2. **The application in one view:** what it does, for whom, and why it matters.
3. **The value path:** the workflow that best explains the application.
4. **System shape:** the smallest architectural view needed to understand that workflow.
5. **What the investigation demonstrated:** observed or tested behavior and important limits.
6. **Consequential findings:** the few findings that most change the current understanding.
7. **Data and integrations:** only the relationships that materially shape the application.
8. **Current build and evolution:** what is working, partial, declared, unused, or missing.
9. **Ownership and operating context:** who uses, owns, and maintains the application when known.
10. **Important unknowns:** what could materially change the current model.

For a compact application, combine related sections. For a complex application, keep the executive story concise and move subsystem detail to an appendix or separate onboarding presentation.
