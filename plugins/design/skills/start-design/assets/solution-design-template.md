## Proposed Solution

## Domain Responsibilities

## Information and Data Flow

## External Systems and Integrations

Describe the important external boundaries and why each relationship exists.

## System Responsibilities

Explain the major system parts and relationships at the level needed to understand the design.

## Important Qualities and Constraints

Explain the qualities and constraints that materially shaped the solution.

## Alternatives and Decisions

Explain the credible alternatives, major decisions, rationale, confidence, and reasons to reopen them.

## Research and Prototype Findings

Include only findings that changed the design or confidence in it. Cite direct sources.

## Remaining Design Questions
