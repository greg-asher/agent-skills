# Scout material

## Sponsor concept

Account executives should receive a weekly ranked list of commercial whitespace, understand why each account appears, investigate supporting evidence, and create or update the right Salesforce record.

The sponsor believes Snowflake contains account, revenue, product, and territory data. Salesforce is described as the system of record for sellers. The concept does not establish which system owns account identity or how conflicts are resolved.

## Seller notes

Two sellers said they do not trust centrally produced lists because account ownership and customer status are often stale. One seller starts with a known customer and looks for missing product adoption; another starts from a territory and searches for accounts.

## Operating note

Sales Operations owns territory assignment. Revenue Operations owns pipeline reporting. The note does not identify who is responsible when Snowflake and Salesforce disagree.

## Older proposal

An earlier slide calls the idea “Lens,” describes managers as the primary user, and says the product will write recommendations directly to Salesforce. The material does not state whether this was superseded.
