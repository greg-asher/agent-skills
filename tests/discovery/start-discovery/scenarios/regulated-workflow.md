# Vendor onboarding concept

A regional US bank is considering an application that coordinates vendor onboarding and ongoing vendor-risk review.

The current process crosses procurement, information security, legal, compliance, finance, and the business sponsor. The initial idea includes AI-assisted document review and risk summaries. The bank has not decided which decisions may be automated or which records constitute the authoritative audit trail.

The discovery participant is an internal technology sponsor. Jurisdiction, bank policy, vendor types, and current system dependencies still need clarification.
