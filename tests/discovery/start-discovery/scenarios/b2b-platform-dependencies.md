# Scout concept note

Commercial leaders believe they miss expansion opportunities inside existing enterprise accounts because useful signals are scattered across systems and interpreted differently by account teams.

Salesforce contains account ownership, opportunities, and seller activity. Snowflake contains product-usage and customer-behavior data. The organization also buys third-party firmographic data, but nobody has established how reliable it is.

The initial idea is “Scout”: an application that identifies commercial whitespace and helps account teams decide where to investigate. It has not been built. The sponsor is the CIO, not an account executive or sales manager.
