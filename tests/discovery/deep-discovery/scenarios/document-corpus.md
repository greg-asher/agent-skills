# Commercial Whitespace source corpus

This is an ordinary folder with no `.git` directory and no application source code.

The supplied material includes:

- `Commercial Whitespace Concept.pptx`: a 12-slide sponsor narrative. Slides 4–7 show a seller moving from a territory signal to account research, manager review, and a Salesforce action. Speaker notes say Snowflake is the proposed analytical source. Slide 10 labels the product name “Scout.”
- `Seller Research.pdf`: 34 pages of interview notes. Pages 18–22 show that sellers begin from known accounts, while sales operations begins from territory gaps. Page 29 says managers distrust opportunity scores they cannot explain.
- `Account Model.xlsx`: sheets named `Accounts`, `Signals`, and `Ownership`. Formulas rank accounts. A comment on `Ownership!D14` says sales operations, not account executives, resolves territory conflicts.
- `working-notes.md`: says the first useful outcome is a reviewable shortlist with reasons and source currency.
- `journey.mmd`: shows signal -> investigate -> shortlist -> manager review -> CRM follow-up.
- `2024-concept.md`: an older proposal that calls the product “Lens,” makes managers the primary user, and assumes a direct Salesforce write. No source says this proposal was formally superseded.
- `sketch.png`: a low-fidelity three-pane experience with signals, evidence, and actions.

The PowerPoint and Word-compatible structure was extracted successfully. The PDF was reviewed in page ranges. The spreadsheet preview omitted 8,200 low-value historical rows but covered formulas, comments, tables, and all nonempty ownership rules. The image was inspected visually.
